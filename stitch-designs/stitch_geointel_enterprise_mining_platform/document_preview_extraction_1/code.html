4.3 Stratigraphic Succession & Lithological Interpretation - Seam IV

Continuous diamond core drilling at station NK-BH-402 (Coordinates: 23°49'12.4" N, 85°03'28.1" E, RL 428.40m) has intersected the cyclic sequence of Gondwana sedimentaries belonging to the Barakar Formation. Borehole log profiles validate the conformable depositional transition over the underlying Talchir glaciomarine contact.
